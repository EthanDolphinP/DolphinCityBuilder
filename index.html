Your areas

Send an expedition

An expedition team maps one new area next to land you already know. The supplies it needs depend on the kind of land it is heading into and how far that is from home. People, materials and research are shared by all your areas.

Research

Research is paid for with Knowledge, which comes from Shaman Huts, Schools and Universities. Each tech level's research needs its research building standing in your city.

Post an offer

I give and want

Every trade is between two cities. What you offer leaves your stock and is held until another city accepts or you cancel. To buy something, offer what you will pay for it.

Bills

Every hours I send to and receive

A bill is a repeating trade with one other city. It starts once they accept, and either of you can cancel it at any time. If either city can't pay when it comes due, nothing changes hands that time; three misses in a row cancel the bill. Active bills are counted in the per-hour figures at the top.

Reading the map

Each tile shows its terrain symbol. If something is on it, the second character is the building (white) or the wild animal (pink). The bar under a building shows how far through its cycle it is, and a + in the corner is an addition. Click any tile for its details.
