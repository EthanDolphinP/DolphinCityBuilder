Eggs and other beasts

How beasts work

Your active beast sets the city one mission at a time. An egg hatches once it has been kept warm long enough and you have completed one mission for it. After that every completed mission gives the beast experience; as it levels up, the bonus it gives the city grows. What the bonus is depends on the kind of beast. Only the active beast sets missions and gives its bonus, and you can change which one is active at any time. Expeditions sometimes bring back more eggs, and rarer kinds are likelier the further from home they go. The Beast Den, Incubator, Training Ground and Beast Sanctuary (each needs research) make all of this faster or stronger.

Your areas

Send an expedition

An expedition team maps one new area next to land you already know. The supplies it needs depend on the kind of land it is heading into and how far that is from home. People, materials and research are shared by all your areas.

Research

Research is paid for with Knowledge, which comes from Shaman Huts, Schools and Universities. Each tech level's research needs its research building standing in your city.

I give and want

Every trade is between two cities. What you offer leaves your stock and is held until it is sold or you cancel. The two amounts set the price, and other cities can buy any part of the offer at that price, a lot at a time. To buy something, offer what you will pay for it.

Finished orders

Offers of yours that sold out. Nothing here is on sale. Reactivate posts one again with the same amounts, taking the goods from your stock again.

Bills
Propose a bill
Every hours I send to and receive

A bill is a repeating trade with one other city. It starts once they accept, and either of you can cancel it at any time. If either city can't pay when it comes due, nothing changes hands that time; three misses in a row cancel the bill. Active bills are counted in the per-hour figures at the top.

Guide

Reading the map

Each tile shows its terrain symbol. If something is on it, the second character is the building (white when working, red when something is wrong, grey when switched off) or the wild animal (pink). The bar under a building shows how far through its cycle it is, and a + in the corner is an addition. Click any tile for its details.
